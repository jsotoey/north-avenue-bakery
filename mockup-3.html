Our story

A Family Tradition

What started as a small dream on North Avenue has become a beloved gathering place for the Elizabeth community.

Our recipes have been handed down through generations of Portuguese bakers. Every morning at 4:30 AM, we begin the day's work — mixing, kneading, and shaping each pastry by hand.
