Our Story

What began as a small family bakery on North Avenue has grown into a beloved community gathering place, known for our authentic Portuguese recipes passed down through generations.

Every morning at 4:30 AM, our bakers begin preparing the day's fresh breads, pastries, and our famous Pastel de Nata — using the same recipes that have been perfected over decades.

The Perfect Nata

Crispy, flaky pastry filled with a rich, creamy custard. Each one crafted by hand, just as they do in Lisbon.
