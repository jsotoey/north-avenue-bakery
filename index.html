Our Story

Three generations of tradition

What started as a family recipe brought from Portugal has grown into Elizabeth's go-to bakery for authentic pastéis de nata and handmade Portuguese pastries.

Every morning, we're here before dawn — rolling dough, layering butter, baking in small batches.

"Every pastry should be as good as the first one our grandmother made."
