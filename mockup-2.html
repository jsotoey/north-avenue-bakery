Our Story

North Avenue Bakery started as a small family dream — bringing the authentic tastes of Portugal to Elizabeth, New Jersey.

Every recipe has been passed down through generations. From our hand-rolled pastry dough to our signature custard filling, we honor the craft that makes Portuguese baking special.

The Famous

Crispy, flaky layers of buttery pastry surrounding a rich, caramelized custard. Made fresh every morning, just as they do in the bakeries of Lisbon.
